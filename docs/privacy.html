GDrive Bridge privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

What it accesses

With your permission, GDrive Bridge uses Google's drive.readonly permission to list the files and folders in your Google Drive and to read the files you choose to play. It also reads your Google account's name and email address, to show which account is signed in.

How the data is used

This information is used only to show your Drive inside Kodi and to stream the files you play to the device the add-on runs on. It is not used for advertising or analytics, not sold, and not shared with anyone.

Where the data is kept

Everything stays on your own Kodi device: the sign-in token, a cache of file and folder names, and the add-on's settings. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any server other than Google's own. Files are streamed from Google to your device and are not stored or copied elsewhere.

Google API Services User Data Policy

GDrive Bridge's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Choose Sign out in the add-on to revoke its access and delete the token from your device. You can also revoke access in your Google Account under Third-party apps & services. Uninstalling the add-on deletes everything it stored.

Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue at https://github.com/GDrive-Bridge/gdrive-bridge/issues.
